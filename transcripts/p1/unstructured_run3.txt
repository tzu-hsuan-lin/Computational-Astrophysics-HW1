Prompt: 
Derive the orbital period of a circular orbit at altitude 550 km above Earth’s surface from Newtonian gravity, and evaluate it in minutes.

Response:
To find the orbital period of a satellite in a circular orbit at an altitude of 550" km" , we can start from fundamental principles of Newtonian mechanics and universal gravitation.
## 1. Derivation of the Orbital Period
Consider a satellite of mass m orbiting Earth (mass M) in a circular orbit of radius r, where:
r=R_E+h
	R_E = Earth's radius
	h = orbital altitude (550" km" )
Step 1: Equate Gravitational Force to Centripetal Force
The gravitational force pulling the satellite toward Earth provides the centripetal force required to keep it moving in a circle:
GMm/r^2 =(mv^2)/r
Where:
	G is the gravitational constant (6.6743×10^(-11) 〖" m" 〗^3 〖"kg" 〗^(-1) "s" ^(-2))
	v is the orbital velocity
Canceling the satellite's mass m and one factor of r gives the expression for orbital velocity:
v=√(GM/r)
Step 2: Relate Velocity to Orbital Period (T)
The distance traveled by the satellite in one complete orbit is the circumference of the circle, C=2πr. The orbital period T is the circumference divided by the orbital velocity:
T=2πr/v
Substitute the expression for v:
T=2πr/√(GM/r)=2πr⋅√(r/GM)
Simplifying this yields Kepler's Third Law for circular orbits:
T=2π√(r^3/GM)
## 2. Numerical Evaluation
Let's plug in the standard physical constants for Earth and the given altitude:
	Earth's Mean Radius (R_E): 6,371" km"=6.371×10^6 " m" 
	Altitude (h): 550" km"=5.5×10^5 " m" 
	Orbital Radius (r): 6,371" km"+550" km"=6,921" km"=6.921×10^6 " m" 
	Standard Gravitational Parameter (μ=GM): 3.986×10^14 〖" m" 〗^3 "s" ^(-2)
Calculation in Seconds:
T=2π√((6.921×10^6 " m" )^3/(3.986×10^14 〖" m" 〗^3 "s" ^(-2) ))
T≈5,730" seconds" 
Conversion to Minutes:
T_"minutes" =5,730" s" /60" s/min" ≈95.5" minutes" 
## Summary
A satellite in a circular orbit at a 550" km"  altitude (commonly used for Earth-observation and constellations like Starlink) completes one full orbit approximately every 95.5 minutes (or about 1 hour and 35.5 minutes).
